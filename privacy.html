Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

LearnOnLock keeps your learning settings and review progress on your devices. The app has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The phrase catalog is bundled with the app.

Speech

Phrases are spoken with the voices installed on your device. Speech is generated on the device.

Apple Watch

A paired Apple Watch receives your selected language and settings from your iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity. That sync stays between your two devices.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used and to fix problems, LearnOnLock can send anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These signals describe how the app is used, for example that a language was picked, a phrase was opened, or a widget was set up. Each device is identified only by an anonymous, hashed identifier that we cannot link to you.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or anything you type.
  • Usage data is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not sold or shared with data brokers.
  • You can turn this off at any time in the app under Settings with the Share anonymous usage stats switch. The choice applies to your Apple Watch too.

Children

LearnOnLock does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
