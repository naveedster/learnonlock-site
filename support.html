How do I add the widget to my Lock Screen?

  1. On your iPhone, touch and hold the Lock Screen until Customize appears, then tap Customize and choose Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area below the clock.
  3. Find LearnOnLock in the list and tap the widget size you want.
  4. Tap Done.

You can also add LearnOnLock widgets to your Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for LearnOnLock. The app shows these steps again under Settings > How to add widgets.

How do I use LearnOnLock on Apple Watch?

Install LearnOnLock on your iPhone, then open the Watch app on your iPhone and make sure LearnOnLock is installed on your watch. Open LearnOnLock on your iPhone once and pick a language; your language and settings are sent to the watch automatically. You can add LearnOnLock as a complication by touching and holding your watch face and tapping Edit.

If the watch says to open LearnOnLock on iPhone, open the iPhone app with the watch nearby and unlocked so they can sync.

A phrase won't play, or the app says a voice is missing

LearnOnLock uses the voices installed on your device. To add one, open Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices, pick the language, and download a voice. Then return to LearnOnLock and try again. Also check that your ringer volume is up and your device isn't in silent mode for media.
