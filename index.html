What's in the beta

Learn in the moments you already have

You check your phone dozens of times a day. Each glance can teach you something.

30 languages, real scripts

Persian, Japanese, Arabic, Spanish, Hindi, Korean and more, each shown in its own writing system.

Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets

The same cream phrase card sits on your Lock Screen and right next to your apps. Tap it and the app opens to that exact phrase.

Script, meaning and sound

Every phrase in its native script with the English meaning, plus a transliteration for non-Latin scripts.

Shuffle

Want another one? Shuffle to a new phrase right from the widget, without opening the app.

Hear it, say it

Tap play to hear the phrase spoken by your iPhone's built-in voices, then say it out loud yourself.

Every minute to daily

Choose how often the phrase changes: every minute, every few minutes, hourly, or once a day.

Review and beginner paths

Mark phrases Again or Got it to review what you've seen, and work through beginner topics in order.

Apple Watch

Phrases on your wrist, with Shuffle and Play. Your language and settings sync from your iPhone.

How it works

Three steps, then just live your day

  1. 1

    Pick a language

    Choose from 30 languages. Phrases stay hidden until you pick one, and you can switch any time.

  2. 2

    Add the widget

    Put LearnOnLock on your Lock Screen, your Home Screen, or both. The Watch app picks up your choice too.

  3. 3

    Learn every time you pick up your phone

    A new phrase shows up as often as you like. Tap it to open the phrase, hear it, and review it later.

An honest note about the phrases

Most phrases are machine-generated and haven't been checked by native speakers yet, so you will find mistakes. If you speak one of the languages, flagging a wrong or unnatural phrase is the most helpful thing you can do. Send the phrase and what you'd actually say to learnonlock@gmail.com.
