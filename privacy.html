Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

LearnOnLock keeps your learning settings and review progress on your devices. The app has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The phrase catalog is bundled with the app.

Speech

Phrases are spoken with the voices installed on your device. Speech is generated on the device.

Apple Watch

A paired Apple Watch receives your selected language and settings from your iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity. That sync stays between your two devices.

No data collection

LearnOnLock does not collect any data. It has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no accounts, and no advertising, and it does not send your learning activity anywhere.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or anything you type.
  • Nothing is used for tracking or advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

Purchases

If you buy LearnOnLock Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

LearnOnLock does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
